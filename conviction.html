Where the data comes from
Universe is the Nifty 500, pulled from NSE each run. prices.json holds 10 years per stock — recent ~2y as daily bars (NSE bhavcopy, appended each evening), older years compressed to weekly bars to keep the file loadable (Yahoo only bootstraps history). fundamentals.json comes from Yahoo Finance statements via yfinance for the names that pass a price pre-filter. overrides.json is the only manual input (moat / leader). A GitHub Action runs update_data.py every weekday evening and commits the files; nothing else to do.

How the score is built

Technical (0–100) — Reversal track: price back above 50-DMA, 20-DMA crossing above 50-DMA, RSI recovering through 50 from <40, MACD histogram turning positive, higher swing lows, bullish engulfing/hammer in last 10 bars. Breakout track: close above 20- and 50-day high, range contraction (last 20-bar range < 60% of prior 40), volume ≥1.5× 20-day average, price within 10% of 52-week high, 50-DMA above 200-DMA. Whichever track scores higher is the stock's signal.
Fundamental (0–100) — revenue growth (3y CAGR), EPS growth, EBITDA/operating margin level and trend, ROE, debt/equity, FCF positivity and FCF/net profit, plus a valuation component (P/E vs. sector median, PEG). Qualitative leadership and moat are a manual 0–5 field in the data.
Blend — Composite = T×w + F×(1−w). Risk tolerance changes the hard gates (min composite, max D/E, min ROE, min market cap multiplier), not the weights.

Data & freshness

Fundamentals are pulled from Yahoo Finance statements via yfinance: TTM where available, otherwise last fiscal year; each stock carries an asOf date and fetched timestamp, shown on the card. Prices cover 10 years per stock (~2y daily + 8y weekly) — daily bars drive the indicators, the weekly history sets multi-year highs and base length. Nothing here is real-time, and Yahoo data for Indian small caps can be stale or wrong — cross-check on Screener.in before acting.
