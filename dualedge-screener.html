Investment criteria (weekly bars)

Stocks that crossed above the 30-week WMA within the last 8 weeks get an EARLY S2 tag — fresh Stage-2 candidates reversing from lows. Pair every pick with a fundamentals check (link in each row).

Bottom-fishing criteria

Proximity to the low only qualifies a stock — the signal needs reversal evidence: a finished base, higher lows, and a reclaimed EMA20. The lookback low is the invalidation level: a close below it kills the thesis, no averaging down. Use the 2-year lookback for the deepest low.

Risk sizing

Stop = close − 2×ATR(14) · Target = close + R × (close − stop) · Qty = (capital × risk%) ÷ (close − stop). At 1:2 you break even at a 33% win rate.

Backtest (swing rules)
